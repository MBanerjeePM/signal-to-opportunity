# DEMO_SCRIPT: Signal → Opportunity (3 minutes)

**Before you start (latency):** the signal analysis takes about a minute and an Internal Review takes about a minute, so run both ahead of time:
1. Upload the synthetic `CustomerSignals.xlsx` and click **Analyze Signals**.
2. Select the PDF page manipulation theme. In Internal Discovery Review, choose **Architect**, enter the feedback below, and click **Add Internal Feedback**. It becomes **IE001**.
3. Click **Run Internal Review** and wait for the result.
4. Do not refresh the page or re-run the analysis, because either clears the feedback.

*Feedback text:* "PDF splitting has permission and document-versioning implications that are different from rotation."

## Spoken script

**0:00–0:25 Problem**
"Product discovery starts with research groundwork: connecting scattered feedback, checking what the product already does, and preparing evidence. We mistake requests for needs, and volume for importance. Signal → Opportunity automates that groundwork, so the Product Manager can focus on judgment."

**0:25–0:40 Upload / Analyze**
"Customer signals come in through Excel today. Everything here is synthetic hackathon test data: CustomerSignals.xlsx and SampleMRG.txt. Reading a real PDF is future work. I ran the analysis ahead of time, because it takes about a minute."

**0:40–1:00 Emerging Opportunities**
"It found two themes, each tied to its signals. Let's open PDF page manipulation."

**1:00–1:35 Discovery Brief**
"Capability validation shows exact quotes and page numbers from the synthetic documentation. Facts are only what customers said, with Signal IDs. Inferences are tagged unvalidated. Counter-evidence challenges our own grouping, and the riskiest assumption is what to test first."

**1:35–2:30 Internal Discovery Review**
"Now internal review. An Architect told us: 'PDF splitting has permission and document-versioning implications that are different from rotation.' We entered a role, not a name. It became IE001, internal expert evidence, kept apart from customer evidence and labelled unverified. I ran the review beforehand.
It classifies the impact as introduces a new constraint, and it lists a new or changed unknown. The original hypothesis stays unchanged.
Then it prepares targeted questions. This one is for customers, about their workflow. This one is internal, to check what the Architect asserted."

**2:30–2:50 Why it's different**
"A generic AI can write a plausible analysis. Here, every claim cites its IDs, and documentation quotes are checked. In one synthetic A/B run, the structured approach demonstrated differentiation on 8 of 10 evaluated dimensions. This is not a benchmark."

**2:50–3:00 Close**
"Automate the research groundwork. Amplify the Product Manager. AI organizes and challenges the evidence. The Product Manager makes the decision."

## DEMO CLICKS
- **0:00:** Results page, banner visible.
- **0:25:** Scroll to Upload Files. Point at both slots and the chosen filename. Don't click Analyze.
- **0:40:** Scroll to Emerging Opportunities. Point at the two themes.
- **1:00:** Click the PDF page manipulation theme. Scroll slowly through Capability Validation (quote and page), Facts, Inferences, Counter-evidence and Riskiest assumption.
- **1:35:** Scroll to the amber Internal Discovery Review panel. Point at the **IE001** card (Architect, feedback text, and no name field), then the impact badge, the "Unverified internal expert assertion" label, Original Hypothesis (unchanged), and New / Changed Unknowns.
- **2:10:** Scroll to Updated Discovery Questions. Point at one **Customer** question, then one **Internal** question.
- **2:30:** Stay on the questions or scroll up to a Signal ID chip. Nothing to click.
- **2:50:** Scroll to the top. Stop.

*Theme names, classifications and wording vary between runs. If the impact label differs from "Introduces new constraint," read the label shown on screen. Use synthetic data only.*
